Our Journey

About BNC Motors

Timeline

2019 — Founded in Bengaluru
BNC Motors incorporated with ₹80 Cr seed funding. Battery R&D lab established at IISc campus.
2021 — BNC Spark Prototype
Spark unveiled at Auto Expo Delhi. 25,000 pre-orders received in 72 hours.
2022 — Manufacturing Plant
200,000-unit annual capacity plant inaugurated in Pune. Spark production begins.
2023 — Storm E-Bike Launch
Performance segment entry with Storm. Series B funding: ₹900 Cr raised from Tiger Global.
2024 — 2 Lakh Scooters on Road
200,000th BNC scooter delivered. Swap network crosses 1,500 stations. Fleet division launched.
2026 — Bolt Pro & Global Expansion
Delivery-first Bolt Pro launched. Entering Vietnam and Indonesia. 3,500+ charge points live.
